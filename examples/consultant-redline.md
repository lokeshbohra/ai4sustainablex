### 1. Scope boundary (🔴)

**Draft says:** "Scope 1 + 2 (market-based) fell from 41,200 tCO₂e (FY2024) to 33,800 tCO₂e (FY2025)."

**Change:** State the consolidation approach (operational control) and that figures are market-based,
with a footnote disclosing location-based numbers if available.

**Why:** A boundary that isn't stated makes the percentage move impossible to audit. The source doc
(p. 7) reports both bases; the draft picked one without saying so.

---

### 2. Scope 3 — do not estimate (🔴)

**Draft says:** *(gap flag — no Scope 3 number)*

**Change:** Keep the gap note. Do not insert an estimate or industry average into the environmental
summary. If Acme must show a number, it should come from the FY2025 CDP submission or a category-by-
category inventory — never from a model.

**Why:** This is exactly where AI-written reports get companies into trouble. A plausible-looking
Scope 3 figure with no methodology is worse than a blank with a disclosure note.

---

### 3. "Net zero by 2045" framing (🟡)

**Draft says:** "Acme has a public target to reach net zero by 2045 [Source 1, p. 12]."

**Change:** Add the qualification that no SBTi validation letter was found in the provided documents,
and note the baseline year and scope coverage of the target (which the annual report states in a
footnote the draft did not surface).

**Why:** Reporting a target without its baseline/scope invites the question the team can't yet answer.

---

### 4. 18% reduction attribution (🟡)

**Draft says:** "which the company attributes to grid decarbonization and fleet electrification."

**Change:** Attribute with the company's own causal language — or drop the attribution. The draft
paraphrased a driver the sources only state as a claim, not a measured decomposition.

---

### 5. Safety claim precision (🟢)

**Draft says:** "without a lost-time incident for 14 months."

**Change:** Confirm whether this covers contractors (usually the larger population at a developer) and
add the reporting period end-date.

**Why:** Scope-of-coverage differences change the number's meaning entirely.

---

### 6. Materiality context (🟢)

**Draft says:** no materiality statement in the summary.

**Change:** Add one line naming the material topics the report addresses (the sources support this).
Boards read the summary first; a materiality anchor tells them what the draft is and isn't claiming.
