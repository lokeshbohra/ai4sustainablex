How it works

Four steps to a draft with receipts

1

Point it at a folder

Your PDFs, DOCX, spreadsheets, slides. Only the folder you choose — nothing outside it.

2

It indexes locally

Documents are chunked and embedded on your machine. No upload, no cloud index.

3

Ask or scaffold

Ask questions against your own files, or pick a structure: Basic ESG, GHG emissions, compliance checklist, and more.

4

Get a draft with receipts

Every claim cited to a file + page. Every gap flagged in place. A grounding score shows how much is backed by your documents.

Then it's your call — not our lock-in: DIY-refine the draft for free, forever. Or when a regulator or board is the audience, add SustainableX Validation: claim-level confidence scoring, ESRS / IFRS S1/S2 data-point checks, peer benchmarking, and a consultant redline with methodology notes.

Trust & privacy

What we deliberately don't fake

  • 🚩"Insufficient data" is a feature. If the number isn't in your documents, the draft says so and logs it as a gap — it never invents one to look complete.
  • 🔖Every claim is traceable. File + page citations on supported claims; severity flags on unsupported ones.
  • 🔒Nothing uploaded. Indexing, search, and default generation run on your machine. Cloud LLMs are optional and opt-in.
  • 🩺Drafts are drafts. The grounding score tells you what needs a human eye. Free drafts are not assurance-ready — that's what Validation exists for.
